How this works
  • What counts: every listing whose price per item is below the item's vendor sell price. "Units" is how many items are listed below that price; "Profit" is what you make buying all of them and selling them to a vendor. Buying on the AH costs no fee or deposit, and vendors take any amount.
  • Small margins add up slowly. Crafted gear is bought one auction at a time and takes a bag slot each, so a few copper per item is rarely worth it. Look at "Profit each" as well as the total.
  • Prices are up to about an hour old. Blizzard publishes an auction snapshot about once an hour, so some of these may already be bought by someone else. Check the auction house before you buy.
  • Vendor prices come from Blizzard's WoW Forever item data. Before buying a lot of something, hover over one in your bags to confirm what a vendor pays.
  • Which items: only the items this site tracks: crafting materials, crafted items, Waylaid Crate materials and Craftsman's Writs. Gear that drops from mobs isn't included.
  • Price history: the ↗ next to an item opens its price history on wowauctions.net.
