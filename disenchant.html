How this works
  • Expected value, not a guarantee. Disenchanting is random. "Disenchants for" is the average over many items: for example a green armor piece gives dust 75% of the time, an essence 20% and a shard 5%. Buy several cheap items rather than counting on one.
  • The odds: green armor 75% dust / 20% essence / 5% shard, green weapons the reverse (mostly essence), blue items always one shard (0.5% chance of a Nexus Crystal from required level 51), purple items 2–4 shards, or Nexus Crystals from required level 51. Which dust, essence or shard depends on the item's required level, in 5-level bands. Odds and amounts are Classic's (warcraft.wiki.gg disenchanting tables); bands follow the WoW Forever disenchanting guide (wow-professions.com), which goes by required level.
  • Any Enchanting skill can disenchant any item in WoW Forever, so you only need to have learned Disenchant.
  • Material prices are the cheapest listing of each dust, essence and shard on your faction's auction house. "What they sell for" takes off the 5% auction house cut; selling a lot at once will push prices down.
  • Prices are up to about an hour old, and the gear list is refreshed every hour. Someone may have bought an item already. Purple items under required level 35 aren't valued (their results aren't documented), and the Arcane Salvager's disenchanting bonus isn't included.
  • Shard planner: blue items always disenchant into one shard (from required level 51 there's a 0.5% chance of a Nexus Crystal instead), and purple items into 2–4 shards (counted as 3). The planner sorts every shard listing and every such item on your auction house by cost per shard and takes the cheapest until you have enough. The gear scan keeps the 60 cheapest blues and purples per shard type, so very large amounts may need more than it shows.
  • Dust and essences: for each auction, cost per unit = (buyout − what the other materials it gives would sell for) ÷ how many of that material it gives on average. "Units at that price or better" adds up the expected amount from every auction that beats buying the material directly. It ignores the quality and profit filters above, but follows "Also buy from".
  • Price history: the ↗ next to an item opens its price history on wowauctions.net.
